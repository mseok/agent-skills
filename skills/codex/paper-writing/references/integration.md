# Integrate the paper around one central message

The coordinator runs this pass after composition and before final correction, including when different writers produced different sections. Keep this guide in the coordinator's context; return necessary scientific direction to the composer through the brief, as specified in `delegation.md`. Compare the draft directly with the author's original message and authorized framing, using the readership and article type established in `venue-adaptation.md`. The message states what the reader should newly understand. Use a provisional message only when the author has supplied none; do not silently replace an author's decision.

## Read the argument in the actual draft

Make a reverse outline from what was written, not what the original plan intended: summarize the new understanding contributed by each core paragraph and the stated relationship that supports it. Distinguish an explanation in the text from a connection you supply using the research record. A topic label or accurate paraphrase of the intended message does not establish that the reader has been given that connection. Supporting paragraphs can name what they support; routine Methods details serve comprehension and reproducibility without restating the central claim.

Read these sentences in order. Check what the reader learns next, why that information belongs here, whether prerequisites arrive before their use and whether the space given to each result matches its importance. Independent tests can stand in parallel. Do not force every result into one causal chain or add repeated slogans to manufacture coherence. Within the authorized scope, repair roles, order, repetition and proportion before polishing transitions.

Judge the whole passage before scoring local features. Does it establish a problem worth solving, make the study's contribution intelligible and sustain a natural progression? A specific opener, early mention of the closest prior method or greater detail is useful only when it improves that progression. During comparisons, keep factual errors separate from editorial preference and do not reward compliance with this skill as evidence of better writing. Preserve the author's preferred framing and accurate prose, making targeted corrections instead of forcing them into the skill's preferred shape. Treat an earlier draft as writing context only when authorized, following `delegation.md`.

In an Introduction, compare the question made consequential by the opening movement with the scientific scope of the contribution. General stakes can establish importance while leaving the reason for this particular question unexplained. Trace the connection through the scientific objects and operations described in the manuscript. When the contribution depends on differences among settings, check that the relevant differences and shared operation are intelligible from the prose. Credit only what the intended reader can understand there, not an explanation available only in the brief or source record.

## Reconstruct when coherence is not enough

Read the draft without its plan and state what it enables, measures or explains for the reader. Compare that answer with the author's original message and the complete record, including relevant prior accomplishments. A fluent sequence of background, literature, components and scores can remain intellectually unresolved. Check whether the question, contribution and final answer refer to the same scientific purpose, and whether controls serve that purpose at the weight their findings warrant.

For a newly requested draft, request a targeted repair when a missing scientific connection prevents understanding the intended contribution, even if the individual statements are accurate and the overall topic is aligned. Such a repair is substantive even when it needs only a few sentences; it need not replace the argument. Give the composer a short instruction naming the missing relationship and the supplied facts that support it. Do not transfer the reverse outline or audit transcript as prose to reproduce. Preserve the supplied purpose unless the evidence requires a change or the author directs one. Keep all facts and material contrary evidence. Do not invent novelty or enlarge the intended claim. For a scoped revision, change only authorized passages and flag a larger mismatch outside them.

An announced weakness in prior work creates a promise: the present study should answer that weakness, or the Introduction should explain its actual role in motivating the question. A performance table does not automatically answer a mechanistic question, and a benchmark extension does not automatically establish a new principle. A useful methodological or empirical advance can carry the paper without that principle. Repair an unsupported novelty claim at its actual scope; preserve the scientific purpose whenever the evidence still supports it.

## Check the sections together

- The Introduction establishes the question and promises an advance that the Results actually address.
- The Methods explain how the approach and comparisons answer that question, with the information needed to interpret and reproduce them.
- The Results build an answer through complementary findings. A later analysis or case adds understanding beyond another score or win.
- The Discussion explains what that answer changes, at the strength the evidence supports. The abstract and title, when supplied, make the same scoped claim.

Read the Introduction's closing argument, the Results headings and core findings, and the Discussion's opening and conclusion together. Check that they communicate the same scientific contribution at a consistent strength. A condition already established in a continuous argument need not be repeated; independently read titles, abstracts and headings must remain accurate on their own. Preserve a contrary result that changes the message. If the evidence requires changing an agreed headline or scope, follow the revision workflow rather than silently rewriting the author's framing.

## Select and finalize

Use the contrasting examples and qualification rule in `register.md` to check what earns space in the prose. Keep useful explanation and interpretation, combine repetition, and place supporting detail where it serves the reader. For an apparently redundant qualification, inspect the surrounding wording that invites the broader inference: repair that wording or retain the necessary boundary. Remove sentences that merely restate verification status, enumerate untested possibilities or defend an already accurate finding. The finished paper should develop its scientific message, not narrate its checks. Keep a coherent draft stable through the separate correction pass; change its argument when a factual problem requires it, not merely because the corrector prefers another presentation. After a substantive repair, check its surrounding argument again.

For a single-section task, apply the pass to the supplied section and surrounding context. Do not expand the edit scope, invent missing sections or claim whole-paper alignment without reading them. Flag a material mismatch outside the authorized passage instead of rewriting it. Keep the reverse outline as working material, not manuscript prose or a mandatory extra deliverable.
