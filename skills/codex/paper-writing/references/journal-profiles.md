# Journal examples: choose by reader and contribution

Checked 26 November 2026. These are editorial interpretations of a purposive corpus: four original research papers per journal, with complete main narratives and opening sections read. They are not frequency estimates or acceptance predictors. Detailed paragraph functions, terminology and reading scope are in the linked analyses. The sample spans 2023–2026; the chemistry subset is a 2024 slice. Author-manuscript and ambiguous Introduction boundaries are identified in the analyses. Refresh formal requirements and select closer examples when the work requires them.

Read only the relevant journal entry and useful matched examples. A scientific citation and a writing example serve different purposes; do not add a source to the manuscript merely because its writing was studied.

## Nature

**Reader and writing decision.** Explain the scientific consequence beyond the immediate method: what can be understood, designed or discovered. Let that consequence determine the starting level and the amount of technical explanation. For a capability paper, make the mechanism explain the expanded scope; for discovery, a motivating hypothesis can carry the opening into the Results. The examples support both strategies. Biological relevance must come from the study's demonstrations.

**Examples:** AlphaFold 3, RFdiffusion, Geneformer, antibiotic-class discovery. See `corpus-nature-biotech.md`.

**Official format:** [Nature formatting guide](https://www.nature.com/nature/for-authors/formatting-guide). Distinguish the fully referenced summary paragraph from the main-text opening. Neither is a universal template for the other.

## Nature Biotechnology

**Reader and writing decision.** Connect the computational or experimental operation to a biological capability, constraint or measurement. Explain why the proposed information can address that constraint and what an informative biological test would show. For integration or selection methods, use the relevant requirements to organize prior work. Terms should retain their experimental meaning: prediction, affinity, activity and clinical utility are different claims. A focused method can have a clear biological purpose without an invented therapeutic promise.

**Examples:** ProGen, antibody evolution, StabMap, Stabl. See `corpus-nature-biotech.md`.

**Official format:** [Content types](https://www.nature.com/nbt/content): Article, 3,000-word main text and 150-word abstract; Introduction unheaded. Recheck before submission.

## Nature Computational Science

**Reader and writing decision.** Make the computational question consequential to scientific reasoning or practice. Explain the relationship between a scientific difficulty and the computation that addresses it. A method paper may need an account of how decisions change; a diagnostic paper may need the competing interpretations that make the measurement informative. Group literature around this relationship. Select results by what they teach the reader, and let the whole argument determine introductory length.

**Examples:** DANTE, MatText, machine-guided path sampling, disease embedding. Their opening blocks range from one to eight prose paragraphs. See `corpus-computational-ai.md`.

**Official format:** [Content types](https://www.nature.com/natcomputsci/content): Article, 3,500-word main text and 150-word abstract; Introduction unheaded. No separate Introduction limit is specified there.

## Nature Machine Intelligence

**Reader and writing decision.** Explain what a representation, learning mechanism or physical implementation changes, and why that change matters for the scientific task. Bring in domain concepts where they explain the computational idea. A hypothesis about representation can organize the argument as well as a performance bottleneck. Contribution placement follows explanatory dependencies; further related work may remain useful after the contribution is introduced. Keep meaningful distinctions such as measured versus projected efficiency.

**Examples:** GOLLuM, molecular sets, quantum meta-design, echo-state graph hardware. See `corpus-computational-ai.md`.

**Official format:** [Content types](https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/content): Article, 3,500-word main text and 150-word abstract. Use the current page rather than older brief guides.

## Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling

**Reader and writing decision.** Let the chemical object, measurement or decision make the problem precise. Explain the methodological distinction at the level a computational chemistry reader needs, and connect it to the use of the resulting prediction or design. A reliability or evaluation problem can be consequential without a new architecture. Organize comparisons by the relevant information, resource or error behavior; do not equate specialist scope with a weak contribution.

**Examples:** MOOD, DrugHIVE, AIRI, active learning for sites of metabolism. See `corpus-chemistry.md`.

**Official scope and format:** [ACS guidelines](https://researcher-resources.acs.org/publish/author_guidelines?coden=jcisd8). Match original research; do not borrow Perspective or Application Note conventions by accident.

## Digital Discovery

**Reader and writing decision.** Connect the chemistry or materials question to the measurement, experiment-selection or automation decision. Make the resource that constrains discovery concrete when relevant, and explain what the method changes about it. A benchmark can answer an information-selection question; an autonomous platform can answer an experimental-practice question. These contributions need different comparisons. Keep terms close to their scientific operations instead of making every study sound like a software platform.

**Examples:** cost-informed reaction optimization, activation-barrier representations, autonomous HPLC, FSL-CP. See `corpus-chemistry.md`.

**Official format:** [Author guidance](https://www.rsc.org/publishing/publish-with-us/publish-a-journal-article/digital-discovery) recommends background that narrows to the research question. Full Papers have no fixed page limit; choose length appropriate to content.
